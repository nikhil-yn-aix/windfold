# study plan

this plan was written before the experiments and extended by the dated items of section 20. this public version uses the paper's terms and leaves out planning notes about the paper and its release, keeping the original section numbers; the hypotheses, decision rules, thresholds and dated items are unchanged.

## 0. what windfold is

**windfold**: how much SCADA a wind farm model needs, and where it can be trusted, mapped before training.

| piece | what it is |
|---|---|
| windfold model | a physics-centered Bayesian model of wake interactions: engineering wake model as calibrated baseline, one wake-interaction correction shared by all turbines in wind-frame coordinates (the shared correction), and shrunk turbine-specific effects. one posterior gives predictions, a coverage map and monitoring thresholds. |
| coverage map | where, by direction, speed and turbine, the data can support a learned correction. the design-only version uses fixed, outcome-independent choices and needs no power values given the supplied directions, speeds and operating pattern; the fitted version uses scales estimated on training data. |
| benchmark | four open SCADA farms through one documented pipeline, fixed split manifests, baseline results, hypotheses fixed in advance |
| tool | a small package: inputs are layout, rotor diameters, a direction-speed record or distribution and an assumed operating pattern (default: all operating); output is the design-only coverage map with declared default scales. callable from OpenOA or FLASC workflows. |

## 3. research questions, hypotheses, endpoints, decision rule

all fixed before any run, recorded with the split manifest and code hash.

| question | hypothesis |
|---|---|
| can common validation mislead for direction shift? | validation misleads: among the baselines (all models except windfold), the one ranked first by time-blocked validation has higher held-out-sector error than the tuned engineering model |
| does representation affect transfer at matched learner? | wind frame transfers: wind-frame inputs beat identity inputs on held-out sectors in each of three matched pairs (penalized linear, GBM, graph network) |
| does coverage add information about where models fail? | coverage predicts failure: the design-only coverage score predicts wind-frame linear's held-out-sector excess error beyond simpler predictors, and flags sectors where it loses to physics better than the best simple predictor |
| does windfold help from the first weeks? | early history advantage: after 30 days of history windfold is more accurate than every identity model and detects injected underperformance better at a matched false-alarm level |

these are the four planned tests; [results.md](results.md) calls them the time-blocked choice, the wind frame, the coverage score and the short history.

endpoint manifest

| | primary metric and scale | data | comparison | success on a farm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| validation misleads | MSE of the centered log relative power, row-weighted over all eligible single-sector splits | development sector splits | d = MSE(baseline ranked first by time-blocked MSE) − MSE(tuned physics); the selection uses time-blocked results only | d > 0 |
| wind frame transfers | same | single-sector splits | three differences MSE(identity side) − MSE(wind-frame side), one per matched pair (section 8) | all three > 0 (intersection-union test, no extra adjustment) |
| coverage predicts failure | wind-frame linear: the linear pair's model with the shared correction only. excess error per split: its sector MSE minus its time-blocked MSE on the same test rows. score: design-only coverage score (section 6) | single-sector and opposite-sector variants | (a) partial Spearman between score and excess error, both rank-residualized on expected wake strength, angular distance to training directions, training rows and the dissimilarity index of Meyer and Pebesma on the wind-frame features; (b) leave-one-farm-out AUC for "wind-frame linear MSE > tuned physics MSE on the sector", logistic map fitted on the other farms, against the best simple predictor chosen on the other farms | (a) > 0 and (b) > 0. if the farm has fewer than 3 sectors in either class, (b) is not estimable and coverage predicts failure is not estimable on that farm |
| early history advantage | (a) MSE on the common test window after 30 days of history, averaged over the 4 starts (SMARTEOLE: its single start). (b) recall within 14 days of injected 5% drops at the training-fixed threshold, with held-out exceedance rate | learning curves; monitoring (section 12) | (a) windfold against the power curve, lookup, cyclic GAM and identity GBM; (b) windfold against the identity GAM and identity GBM monitors | (a) all four differences > 0; (b) recall difference > 0, and the upper 95% bound of the exceedance difference below 0.5 percentage points, for both monitors |

"> 0" means the 95% interval from the synchronized day-block bootstrap (section 11) lies above zero. each component gets a one-sided bootstrap p-value; a hypothesis's p-value on a farm is the largest of its required components' p-values (intersection-union), and the four hypothesis p-values are Holm-adjusted per farm.

decision rule: a hypothesis is supported if it succeeds on at least 3 of the development farms where it is estimable (at least 3 must be estimable) and on every estimable confirmation dataset, with at least one confirmation dataset estimable. otherwise it is reported as not supported or not estimable. the four answers are reported whichever way they come out; the benchmark and the measured answers stand either way.

secondary, with intervals, outside the decision rule: non-inferiority of windfold after 365 days (upper bound of the relative MSE difference to the best baseline below 2%) on farms with a 365-day horizon; calibration of predictive intervals; the opposite-sector against count-matched-control deletion contrast (section 7).

the estimand: learning curves measure how accuracy depends on the amount of power data used to fit models, conditional on frozen preprocessing and configurations: sensor calibration, fitted power curves and hyperparameters come from the calibration windows (plus causal direction-only offsets); for SMARTEOLE, and for Hill of Towie's published northing, the sensor calibration is the providers' retrospective one, disclosed as such.

this is conditional estimation of a turbine's power given its peers at the same time, the setting of neighbor-based monitoring and lost-production methods; it is not forecasting.

## 6. the method: windfold

raw model for turbine i at time t:

log p_ti = μ_t + α_i + β φ_ti + Σ_j g(a_ij(θ_t), c_ij(θ_t), s_t) + v_{i, b(θ_t), k(s_t)} + e_ti, with e_ti independent N(0, σ²).

μ_t is a common level per timestamp (weather) that the centering removes; a and c are along-wind and across-wind distances in rotor diameters; b is a 5 degree direction bucket and k one of the 3 speed bins.

| term | meaning | prior |
|---|---|---|
| α_i | turbine offset | Gaussian, wide; offsets sum to zero (the centering removes the common level) |
| β φ_ti | engineering wake model: φ_ti = log of the PyWake power of turbine i at (θ_t, s_t) with the actual operating turbines, minus log of its free-stream power at s_t, floored at 1% of rated; variant chosen by training MSE among 14 (Jensen, Bastankhah-Porté-Agel, TurbOPark; per-turbine hub heights); evaluated on the actual operating set, cached by direction, speed bin and set | Gaussian centered at 1 |
| g | the shared correction: one wake-interaction term shared by all turbines and directions, summed over valid upstream neighbors within 30 rotor diameters along and 3 across; knots on a fixed geometric grid | tensor B-spline coefficients with precision q = λ(difference penalty + εI), so the induced Gaussian process kernel is k(r, r') = b(r)ᵀq⁻¹b(r') |
| v | turbine x direction bucket x speed bin local effect (terrain, sensor bias) | exchangeable Gaussian, variance τ² |

likelihood: at each timestamp the m_t valid turbines are projected onto an orthonormal basis of contrasts (Qᵀ1 = 0), which removes μ_t and leaves independent N(0, σ²) noise in m_t − 1 dimensions. this is the exact Gaussian likelihood of the centered data; all posteriors are conditional on the empirical-Bayes scales σ², λ, τ², fitted by marginal likelihood on training data only.

outputs

- prediction: posterior mean. unsupported corrections shrink toward zero, so predictions lean on the calibrated physics term there; shrinkage, not a hard gate or a guarantee. physics, shared and local terms can trade off; the learned correction is not presented as a uniquely identified physical wake.
- predictive variance of the leave-one-out contrast: [m/(m−1)]²(x_i V x_iᵀ + σ²H_ii), with V the coefficient covariance and H = I − 11ᵀ/m; cross-covariances kept when residuals are aggregated over a day.
- design-only coverage score: for each test row, Σ_j k_phys(r_ij)²·Var_post[g(r_ij)] / Var_prior[g(r_ij)], averaged over the rows of a sector, with fixed declared scales (λ0, σ0), the shared correction's block only and the untuned default physics single-wake kernel k_phys as weight. it is a diagnostic, distinct from the full centered predictive variance. it needs the directions, speeds and operating masks but no power values (the masks themselves come from a pipeline that reads power). the fitted coverage map (empirical-Bayes scales, all blocks) is reported separately.
- monitoring threshold: section 12.

three propositions

1. half-turn symmetry: for a fixed set of reversible turbine pairs and one common rotor diameter, the multiset of pair relations at θ+180 equals that at θ with roles swapped.
2. identifiability: for the unrestricted linear mean, a test prediction is determined by noiseless training predictions if and only if its centered design row lies in the row space of the centered training design. an identity cell without training rows is not identified: for a bounded class containing indistinguishable nonzero centered contrasts, such as the two-point class {+v, −v}, the minimax risk is at least v²; this does not extend to smooth identity models such as GAMs. a wind-frame shared term is identified when its relations are spanned, wherever in the farm they were seen; an unseen local cell stays unidentified.
3. design-only coverage: given fixed scales, the posterior covariance depends only on the design, so the coverage score needs no power values; an unsupported correction keeps its prior mean apart from its posterior covariance with supported blocks, which is reported.

## 7. splits and leakage control

| regime | definition | why |
|---|---|---|
| random | 5 folds over timestamps | what most published SCADA models report; shown as optimistic |
| time-blocked | 5 contiguous month-aligned folds with 7-day buffers (SMARTEOLE: 4 contiguous blocks of equal length with 3-day buffers) | the stricter common practice; right for temporal interpolation |
| sector-blocked | 12 non-overlapping 30 degree test sectors; eligible with at least 300 turbine rows and 10 distinct days (timestamps, rows and days always reported). training drops the sector plus 5 degrees each side, in three variants: sector only; sector and opposite sector; sector and a count-matched control set of other directions (removed rows within 5%, speed-bin and month mix matched by stratified sampling; sectors that cannot be matched are declared unmatched, not relaxed) | controlled direction shift; separates coverage from data amount |
| learning curves | training [start, start + H days) for H in 7, 14, 30, 60, 91, 182, 365, then at least 7 days gap, then one common test window. Kelmarsh: starts 2017-01-01, 04-01, 07-01, 10-01; test 2019. Penmanshiel: starts 2017-04-01, 07-01, 10-01, 2018-01-01; test 2019-01-08 to 2019-12-31. Hill of Towie: starts 2023-09-01, 12-01, 2024-03-01, 06-01; test 2025-06-08 to 2025-12-31. SMARTEOLE: one start at 2020-02-17 16:30; H in 7, 14, 30, 60; test 2020-04-25 to 2020-05-24 | data readiness without a moving test set |
| confirmation | the whole study rerun once on Penmanshiel 2020 to 2023: random, time-blocked and sector-blocked splits on 2020 to 2023; learning curves from 2020-01-01, 04-01, 07-01, 10-15 with test year 2022 (2023 never enters learning-curve fitting); coverage predicts failure's leave-one-farm-out map fitted on the other three farms only | replication on data never used for model decisions |

a training window with fewer than 300 retained timestamps (likely SMARTEOLE at 7 days, and the Hill of Towie 2023-12-01 start at 7 days) is reported as "insufficient data", decided after the final pipeline and before any model result; a horizon's average is over the starts that are usable.

hyperparameters: GBM and graph-network configurations come from one common menu of 30 configurations (seeded, the same for every farm), searched once per farm on its calibration window (time-ordered 80/20 split inside it) and then fixed for every split and history of that farm; SMARTEOLE uses the configuration with the best mean rank across the other farms' searches, ties broken by menu order. penalized linear models (windfold, GAM, ablations) choose penalties by marginal likelihood on each training set. physics variants are chosen by training MSE in each training set. no inner validation inside outer splits is needed.

| leakage route | control |
|---|---|
| temporal autocorrelation | time-blocked buffers; learning-curve gaps; day-block bootstrap |
| direction noise at sector edges | 5 degree training buffer (0 and 10 as sensitivity) |
| power-based preprocessing | northing, missing power curves and hyperparameters fitted on calibration windows that are never tested; published curves for outlier and icing bounds |
| per-turbine direction offsets, jump detection | causal trailing windows on direction signals only |
| spline knots, feature scaling | fixed geometric grids declared in advance, not fitted to data |
| model choices | penalties by marginal likelihood and physics variants on training data only; refit on all training data |
| kW conversion | lognormal correction from training residual variance only |
| monitoring thresholds | cross-fitted residuals inside the training window (section 12) |
| the turbine's own power and speed | leave-one-out peer contrast; a waked turbine's anemometer never enters s_t; an input-isolation test checks that perturbing a waked target's nacelle speed leaves its prediction unchanged |
| contemporaneous peers | part of the task definition, stated explicitly (as in neighbor-based monitoring) |
| development decisions | Penmanshiel 2020 to 2023 gets one frozen predictive evaluation with no outcome-driven changes; its earlier metadata and quality checks are disclosed; split manifest and code hash recorded beforehand |
| summary code | unit tests on synthetic results with known answers, including missing split fields; tables only from complete matched outputs |
| injected faults | preprocessing and peer masks frozen before injection; contrasts recomputed consistently |

## 8. models compared

| family | model | name | why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|
| reference | no-wake constant per turbine | no wake, `no_wake` | the floor any useful model must beat |
| identity | per-turbine power curve (speed bins only) | power curve, `power_curve` | the simplest expected baseline |
| identity | direction lookup per turbine, speed bin and 5 degree bucket | direction lookup, `direction_lookup` | monitoring practice (energy-ratio tables) |
| identity | cyclic GAM per turbine (direction harmonics up to order 4, by speed bin), Gaussian posterior | cyclic GAM, `cyclic_gam` | strongest smooth classical identity model; gives predictive intervals |
| identity | GBM on turbine ID, direction, speed (LightGBM, demeaned squared loss) | identity GBM, `identity_gbm` | strongest tabular ML |
| wind frame | windfold's engine with only the shared correction (no physics, no local term) | wind-frame linear, `kernel_linear` | the representation alone; the linear pair and coverage predicts failure |
| wind frame | GBM on wind-frame geometry features and speed, no ID, no absolute direction | wind-frame GBM, `geometry_gbm` | same learner as identity GBM, inputs swapped |
| absolute frame | graph network: all turbine pairs within 30 diameters as edges with (distance, sin and cos of absolute bearing); node inputs reference speed and sin and cos of farm direction (Daenens 2025 style, without own-anemometer inputs) | absolute graph, `absolute_graph` | the current deep model class on SCADA |
| wind frame | the same graph network with edges (along, across) in the wind frame and node input reference speed only; invariant to rotating layout and wind together | wind-frame graph, `wind_frame_graph` | same architecture, frame swapped |
| physics | PyWake, 14 variants, selected on training data | PyWake, `pywake` | engineering baseline and windfold's prior |
| hybrid | physics plus per-turbine cyclic discrepancy over direction and speed, Gaussian posterior | PyWake with discrepancy, `pywake_discrepancy` | the identity-keyed hybrid, in the spirit of Andersson 2020; gives predictive intervals |
| hybrid | GBM on blockage ratio, inverse blocking distance and physics efficiency | physics-guided GBM, `physics_guided_gbm` | physics-guided ML in the style of Zehtabiyan-Rezaie 2023 |
| ours | windfold | windfold, `windfold` | the method |

matched pairs (identical target, rows, loss, solver or architecture, tuning budget and seeds; only the representation differs)

| pair | identity side | wind-frame side |
|---|---|---|
| penalized linear | windfold's engine with α_i and a per-turbine cyclic direction block, no physics, no local term (identity linear, `identity_linear`) | windfold's engine with α_i and the shared correction, no physics, no local term (wind-frame linear, `kernel_linear`) |
| GBM | turbine ID, direction, speed | wind-frame geometry features, speed |
| graph network | absolute edges and farm direction at nodes | wind-frame edges, no absolute direction anywhere |

runs: GBM and graph networks with 3 seeds (mean and spread). graph networks run on the random, time-blocked and single-sector splits (validation misleads, wind frame transfers); GBM also on learning curves (early history advantage); opposite and control deletion variants run for the linear models (coverage predicts failure and the mechanism analysis).

## 9. ablations, one factor at a time

| ablation | question |
|---|---|
| no physics term | does physics carry the weakly supported relations? |
| no shared correction | does sharing across turbines and directions carry the transfer? |
| no local term | do site effects matter once data cover them? |
| turbine positions permuted in the shared correction's features only; physics, validity and test rows fixed | does the real geometry carry the learned gain? |
| default physics instead of tuned | how much does the fallback depend on physics quality? |

supplement: fixed shrinkage instead of marginal likelihood; binned shared correction instead of splines; no direction calibration; window of the shared correction (20, 30, 40 diameters along; 2 or 3 across); sector buffer 0, 5, 10 degrees; 60 degree sectors; speed window 4 to 12 m/s.

## 10. outcomes and metrics

| outcome | metric |
|---|---|
| accuracy | MSE of centered log relative power and skill against the no-wake reference (1 − MSE/MSE_ref), on waked rows (headline) and all rows; MAE in kW and % of rated power, same conversion for every model |
| wake shape | energy ratio per waked turbine and 5 degree bin: Σ p_i / Σ p_ref over the bin's rows, with p_ref the mean power of free-stream turbines (FLASC definition); error of predicted against observed ratio |
| validation agreement | Kendall rank correlation of model rankings between random, time-blocked and sector-blocked validation |
| uncertainty | CRPS; empirical coverage of 90% and 99% intervals; interval width (windfold, cyclic GAM, physics plus discrepancy) |
| monitoring | held-out exceedance rate on uninjected data; coverable share; recall within 14 days and Kaplan-Meier median delay (right-censored at 14 days) for injected 2% and 5% drops |
| coverage | partial rank correlation with excess error; AUC (coverage predicts failure) |
| confounding | results by speed bin, month and season, time of day (a weak stability proxy) and turbulence intensity where available; residual stability and terrain confounding acknowledged |

## 11. statistics

- per-farm results first; split configurations are never treated as independent; heterogeneity across farms reported.
- 95% intervals from a synchronized day-block bootstrap: 7-day blocks (1 and 14 as sensitivity), the same resampled blocks for every model, paired variant and injected copy.
- primary inference: the endpoint manifest of section 3, Holm-adjusted over the four hypotheses per farm, and the decision rule. no other significance tests. "practically equivalent" only with a stated tolerance.

## 12. application: monitoring from the first weeks

- histories of 30 days and longer (shorter ones: accuracy only, declared in advance).
- thresholds from a held-out calibration split inside the training window of H days: fit on [0, 0.75H), leave a gap [0.75H, 0.8H), compute leave-one-out residuals on [0.8H, H) (30 days: fit days 0 to 22.5, calibrate days 24 to 30), then refit on the whole window for test predictions. per turbine, the standard deviation σ_r and lag-1 autocorrelation ρ of the 10-min calibration residuals give the standard deviation of a daily mean of n rows as σ_r·sqrt((1 + ρ)/((1 − ρ)·n)); an alarm fires when the standardized daily mean falls below the one-sided normal quantile for one false alarm per turbine-month (a nominal target under this model, not a verified bound). a day needs at least 36 valid 10-min rows. a turbine with fewer than 3 such calibration days is not monitored at that history; if fewer than half the turbines can be monitored, that farm and history are not estimable.
- the same procedure for every monitor: windfold, identity GAM, identity GBM, physics. the 20-row lookup with abstention is reported as a policy with its coverable share, not as the only identity monitor.
- injections: 50 matched copies of the common test window per farm, history, start and drop size (2% and 5%); in each copy one random eligible turbine loses that share of power for 14 consecutive days starting on a random day; preprocessing and peer masks frozen before injection. "healthy" is an operational screening assumption, and the drops are isolated-turbine tests: relative-power monitoring cannot see a drop shared by every turbine.

label-free illustration: probability that each sector has enough support in both arms of a control trial after n days, from a weekly block bootstrap of the multi-year direction-speed record with the Hill of Towie 50-minute toggle schedule and availability. a planning aid, not a guaranteed duration.

## 14. verification and reproducibility

- by hand per farm: raw series, calibration plots, row counts; split integrity tests (no test day in any training statistic; learning curves never see the future); the input-isolation test of section 7.
- metric and summary code tested on synthetic data with known answers.
- model checks: a small additive generative model with a known shared correction on the real layouts and wind records tests recovery and interval calibration; PyWake-generated data used only as a non-additive realism check.
- an independent audit of numbers, code and claims before reporting.
- uv lockfile, one command per table and figure, fixed seeds, hashes of data, split manifests and results.

## 18. claims we will not make, things we will not do

- no "first" claims; no claims beyond the farms studied; no claim that coverage guarantees accuracy; no universal number of days; no absolute energy-yield, bankable uplift or control-gain claims; no claim that the learned shared correction is the physical wake.
- not doing: random or time-blocked validation alone; SDWPF for anything direction related; forecasting or persistence baselines; metaheuristic tuning; MAPE on power; bootstrapping single rows; a waked turbine's own anemometer as input; skipping northing; rank tests or critical-difference diagrams over four farms; model-based new-farm uncertainty presented as accuracy; wake-steering periods; day-zero transfer between farms; retrofit analyses; extra significance tests outside the manifest.
